I am a third-year PhD candidate in Economics at Ghent University with interests in environmental, energy, and transport economics. My research empirically examines supply and demand interactions in electric vehicle markets and the flexibility of EV charging demand.

Before joining Ghent University, I was a consultant at the World Bank in the Development Economics Policy Indicators Group (DECIG) and a research assistant at the European Organisation for Rare Diseases (EURORDIS).

I hold a master's degree in Economics from the Barcelona School of Economics and a bachelor's degree in Economics from the Universitat de Barcelona.
